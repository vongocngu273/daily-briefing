# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
One reader: the owner, reading a personal Vietnamese-language briefing. Reads on any device (iPhone via the Telegram link in the morning, but also iPad/Mac and at other times of day). Some days a 2–3 minute skim of weather and the biggest headlines; other days a deeper read opening many "Xem đầy đủ" sections.

## Product Purpose
A daily static page (GitHub Pages, rebuilt 07:10 Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh) that answers: what is the weather in my area today, how did markets close, and what happened in the world, Vietnam, tech/science and society that I should know. Success: the reader wants to open it every morning and can stop at any depth (weather → 3 takeaways → headlines → full details) without losing the thread.

## Positioning
Not a news site: a curated, deduplicated digest. Deterministic data (Open-Meteo, Yahoo Finance closes, RSS from VnExpress, Tuổi Trẻ, VietnamPlus, BBC, Reuters/AP/Bloomberg via Google News) curated by Gemini into 3 layers × 3 categories, max 5 items each, with an original-source link per item and an "Cập nhật" tag for follow-ups to already-published stories.

## Operating Context
- Telegram message at ~07:12 links to the page with `?v=YYYYMMDD`.
- Weather: 3 windows (Sáng 07–09, Trưa 11–14, Chiều tối 17–20), temperature range, sky, rain %, rain mm, UV, 1–2 advice lines.
- Market: 9 instruments (Dow, S&P 500, Nasdaq, Nikkei, VN-Index, gold, Brent, USD/VND, BTC) with close + % change; "Chưa có dữ liệu" when missing; SJC gold is a link only.
- Severity per item: 🔴 / 🟡 / ⚪.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Single self-contained static HTML from a Jinja2 template (`templates/index.html.j2`), autoescaped; no external JS; works offline after load.
- Mobile-first; automatic light/dark via `prefers-color-scheme`; `tabular-nums` for figures.
- Spec bans: cards, gradients, glow.
- Required structure: header with update time and quick links (Thời tiết, Thị trường, 3 điều cần nhớ, Mở/Thu gọn tất cả); weather block; table of contents for 3 layers; each item as `<details><summary>Xem đầy đủ ▾</summary>`; market table at the top of the economy category; "3 điều cần nhớ hôm nay" at the end.
- Content volume varies daily (0–45 items); categories may be empty.

## Brand Commitments
Personal and separate: must NOT use the shop (Tiệm Sửa Quần Áo Chú Thịnh) branding.

## Evidence on Hand
Real daily content is generated by the pipeline; no testimonials or claims needed. AI summaries can be wrong, so the page keeps a disclaimer and original links.

## Product Principles
1. Every depth is a valid stopping point: weather and takeaways first-glance, details on demand.
2. Facts over decoration: numbers are exact or explicitly missing, never invented.
3. A morning ritual the reader looks forward to, not a chore to scan.
4. The source is always one tap away.
